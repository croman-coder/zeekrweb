ZEEKR 7X frequently asked questions

What is the range of the ZEEKR 7X?

Up to 480 km (WLTP) in the Smart version with a 75 kWh battery and up to 543 km (WLTP) in the Performance version with a 100 kWh battery. Real-world range varies with weather, road, load and driving style.

What warranty does the ZEEKR 7X have in Paraguay?

5 years or 100,000 km for the vehicle and 8 years or 160,000 km for the battery, whichever comes first.

Which ZEEKR 7X versions are available?

Smart (421 hp, 0–100 km/h in 6.0 s, 480 km WLTP) and Performance (646 hp, 0–100 km/h in 3.8 s, 543 km WLTP).
